Privacy

ERGatory runs in your browser. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your rides (the recorded .fit files and their summaries), your notes, RPE and feel, your settings (FTP, weight, preferences), the workouts you add, and the list of Bluetooth devices you paired. They are kept in this browser's own storage and are never sent to us. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them.

What leaves your device, and only if you ask

intervals.icu. If you paste your own intervals.icu API key into Settings, it is stored in this browser, and ERGatory uses it to send your finished rides (with their name, RPE, feel and notes) to your intervals.icu account and to read your planned workouts from it. That traffic goes straight from your browser to intervals.icu; ERGatory's server never sees it. Remove the key in Settings to stop.

Files you download (a ride's .fit or image) go wherever you save them.

What the server sees

The public ERGatory is a set of static files served by Cloudflare. Loading it is an ordinary web request, which Cloudflare handles under its privacy policy. ERGatory itself keeps no logs and stores nothing about you. The page's font is loaded from Google Fonts, which sees that request.

The household copy (a separate, private address) additionally sends debug traces of its use to its own server and reads one room's sensors from a home automation system. The public version does neither.
